## Known image quality limitation
Reference-proportioned assets have native widths as low as 480px. On large monitors, the full-size artwork will appear softer than the original high-resolution generated masters. If fidelity permits, replace low-resolution images with quality-equivalent PokeETH art of exactly the same aspect ratio. Do not stretch 4:3 artworks into 16:9, crop mascot faces, or add extra overlaid characters over already-complete narrative illustrations.
